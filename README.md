# James Ades

Data scientist in San Diego. I build data science tools and apps.

I hold a Master of Science in Data Science and Engineering from the University of California, San Diego, and have worked as a data scientist at UC San Diego Health and as a research associate at NEAT Labs.

## Apps

- **[Budget](https://github.com/james-ades/budget-app)**: a private budget tracker with monthly totals by category and a six-month view.
- **[Time Tracker + Pomodoro](https://github.com/james-ades/work_tracking)**: a time tracker with a built-in Pomodoro timer and a daily timeline.
- **[Adaptive Typing Trainer](https://github.com/james-ades/typing_app)**: a typing trainer that finds your weak keys and drills them.
- **[learning_app](https://github.com/james-ades/learning_app)**: graph-based spaced repetition, studied by reconstructing a graph of ideas from memory.
- **[Better Habits](https://github.com/james-ades/better-habits)**: a daily habit tracker for iPhone and Mac.

## Data science

- **[spatial_package](https://github.com/james-ades/spatial_package)**: functions for spatial analyses.
- **[fspackage](https://github.com/james-ades/fspackage)**: data quality checks on FSP, in R.
